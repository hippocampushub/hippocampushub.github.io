Hippocampus Hub

Discover a unique set of resources for hippocampus researchers and learners to build models using powerful modeling workflows, and to interactively explore models of the hippocampus and its components!

About the Hub

The Hippocampus Hub brings together free research tools and services, and learning resources for scientists and students interested in the hippocampus. It aims to provide a unique resource and bring together those interested in the hippocampus to create an active community.

Who Is the Hub For?

The Hub is designed for everyone — from students and experimental scientists to computational neuroscientists and curious members of the public. We welcome diverse users and aim to inspire contributions from experimental scientists and modelers alike.

Collaborate With Us

Have a project idea or want to collaborate? We're eager to hear from you! Whether you're contributing data or advancing models, your input is valuable. Visit our Terms & Conditions for more details on how to collaborate with us.

Developed by

The Hub is a joint initiative by the Institute of Biophysics, National Research Council (Italy), and the Blue Brain Project, École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (Switzerland).

Our Tools

The Hub offers two primary tools: a powerful platform for building new neuroscience models, and an interactive explorer for delving into previously constructed models.

Explore Models

Explore resources for building detailed hippocampal models, with tools for collaborative creation and visualization in an open-access environment. Utilize neural data from platforms like NeuroMorpho and Hippocampome, and seamlessly integrate with EBRAINS services for comprehensive model development and analysis.

Build Models

Access the data behind published models, review model artifacts, and browse validation results — all without needing to download files or install software. You can also run in silico experiments using online services and computational resources provided by e-Science platforms like EBRAINS.
